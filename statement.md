# Smart College Expense Tracker

## 1. Problem Statement

College students often have to manage expenses related to food, transportation, shopping, education, subscriptions, and other daily activities. When expenses are recorded manually or not tracked regularly, it becomes difficult to understand spending habits, control unnecessary expenses, and manage a monthly budget.

**Smart College Expense Tracker** aims to provide a centralized financial management application designed specifically for college students. The system allows users to record and manage expenses, set budgets, analyze spending patterns, track shared expenses, and monitor their overall financial habits through a single application.

The project focuses on making personal expense management simple, organized, and useful for students while providing an easy-to-use and responsive interface.

## 2. Scope of the Project

The scope of the Smart College Expense Tracker includes the development of a student-focused expense management application with modules for expense tracking, budget management, analytics, recurring expenses, expense splitting, reports, alerts, and spending analysis.

The project uses **Java and Spring Boot for the backend**, with a modern web-based frontend and a MySQL database for storing user and financial information.

The system also includes a **Student Spending Score** that evaluates financial habits based on factors such as budget utilization, discretionary spending, category discipline, and retained savings.

The current project is mainly intended for academic, learning, and demonstration purposes. It uses user-entered financial information and does not directly connect to banking or payment systems.

## 3. Target Users

The system is designed for users who may need to manage and monitor their personal expenses, including:

* College students
* University students
* Hostel students
* Students living away from home
* Students managing monthly allowances
* Students sharing expenses with friends or roommates
* Students and academic users for demonstration and learning
